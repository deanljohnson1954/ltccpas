The Financial Reality in California

What Extended Care Actually Costs

A monthly figure is difficult to judge in isolation. The same number over a realistic period of care is not.

One Year$180,000
Three Years$540,000
Five Years$900,000

Approximate, at a Bay Area private nursing home room of roughly $15,000 a month. A semi-private room runs closer to $12,000, and in-home care varies with the hours required.

These are today’s costs. They do not allow for future increases — and roughly one in five people who need care will need it for more than five years.

The Case for a Review

Why Review an Existing Policy?

Insurance products change. So do tax laws. So do your financial goals. A policy purchased fifteen or twenty years ago may no longer represent the most efficient way to protect your family.

A professional review may identify opportunities to:

Improve benefits
Reduce premiums
Increase death benefits
Add living benefits
Improve tax efficiency
Coordinate coverage with your estate plan

Sometimes the recommendation is to keep exactly what you own. Independent advice means the recommendation follows the facts — not a sales quota.

Every Significant Insurance Policy Deserves a Second Opinion.

Just as major financial statements are independently audited, significant insurance decisions deserve an objective review. You may discover that your existing coverage remains the best choice. Or you may uncover opportunities that were unavailable when your policy was originally purchased. Either way, you will know.
